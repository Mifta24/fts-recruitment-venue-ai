# Admin CMS (`/admin`)

- **Dashboard:** pending requests (and those waiting more than 24 h), open handovers, events starting in the next 7 days, utilisation over 30 days.
- **Spaces:** CRUD for space types (capacity, breakout rooms, booths, floors, seating layouts, atmosphere, catering, technical crew add-on, price, amenities, photos by URL) and **Inventory**: open a date range with a number of spaces and a price per day; a monthly calendar shows free/total per day.
- **Knowledge base:** CRUD for entries in three languages.
- **Bookings:** list with organiser, company, event type, dates and total; `pending → confirmed | cancelled`, `confirmed → cancelled`.
- **Handovers:** conversations the coordinator passed to staff; staff reply directly and resolve.
- **Settings (owner only):** contact details, opening hours, language, time zone, package discounts, draft/published.
